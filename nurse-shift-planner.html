Frequently asked questions

Can I plan annual leave with this?

Yes — open 3, 6 or 12 months ahead and look for long off-blocks. But confirm with your ward’s leave system: this tool only repeats the pattern you entered.

How do night shifts show?

A night belongs to the date it starts on. If your night runs 20:00 Tuesday to 08:00 Wednesday, it shows as Tuesday’s shift everywhere — calendar, counts, lookup and .ics.

My ward does self-rostering. Will this help?

Yes for planning, with a caveat: self-rosters can change month to month. Use the calendar to model your requested line, and update the pattern or anchor if your confirmed roster changes.

Is my roster data sent anywhere?

No. There is no server and no account — your pattern stays in your browser and in any share link you choose to copy.

Can I add it to my phone calendar?

Yes. Export a .ics of the months shown — one event per working shift, timezone-correct across daylight saving — and import it into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.
