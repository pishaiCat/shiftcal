Continental Shift Calendar — Free 28-Day Early/Late/Night Rotation

The Continental is a fast forward-rotating 28-day schedule common in European factories and 24/7 plants. Four teams move through short blocks of 8-hour shifts — Early (06:00–14:00), Late (14:00–22:00) and Night (22:00–06:00) — rotating forward Early → Late → Night, with days off after each night block. Each team works 21 shifts and gets 7 days off per cycle.

How the Continental pattern works

  • Days 1–9: 2 Early, 2 Late, 3 Night, 2 Off
  • Days 10–18: 2 Early, 3 Late, 2 Night, 2 Off
  • Days 19–28: 3 Early, 2 Late, 2 Night, 3 Off
  • That is 2+2+3+2 = 9, then 2+3+2+2 = 9, then 3+2+2+3 = 10 — 28 days. The rotation always moves forward: day → late → night.

Frequently asked questions

What does “forward rotation” mean?

You move from Early to Late to Night, in the same direction as your body clock travels. It is generally considered easier on the body than rotating backwards.

How many shifts do I work per cycle?

21 of 28 days — 7 Early, 7 Late and 7 Night, with 7 days off.

Why are the blocks only 2–3 days long?

That is what makes the Continental “fast-rotating”: you never spend more than 3 consecutive days on nights before the rotation moves on.

How do I anchor my pattern?

Take one date you know for certain — “on 8 June I worked a Late, day 5 of the pattern” — and enter date + position in the generator. One certain fact fixes every other date.

Can I export it to my calendar?

Yes — the .ics export creates one event per working shift with your timezone, so Early/Late/Night blocks appear at the right wall-clock times across daylight-saving changes.
