Panama / 2-2-3 Shift Calendar — Free 14-Day Rotation Calendar

The Panama schedule — also called the 2-2-3 — is the classic 12-hour shift pattern for 24/7 operations: emergency services, manufacturing, energy and security. Two on, two off, three on, two off, two on, three off, repeating every 14 days. Every other weekend is a full three-day weekend off.

How the Panama / 2-2-3 pattern works

  • Your repeating sequence is 14 days long: 2 working days, 2 off, 3 working days, 2 off, 2 working days, 3 off.
  • No crew ever works more than 3 shifts in a row, and each 14-day cycle contains a built-in 3-day break.
  • Week 1 and week 2 are mirror images, so “every other weekend off” is baked into the rhythm.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours do I work per week on 2-2-3?

With 12-hour shifts: 36 hours one week, 48 the next, averaging 42 hours per week.

What is the difference between Panama and Pitman?

The on/off rhythm is the same (2-2-3). Panama is usually the rotating version where crews cycle between days and nights; Pitman usually keeps crews fixed on days or nights for a stretch.

How do night shifts appear on the calendar?

A night shift always belongs to the date it starts on. A 19:00 Tuesday night finishing 07:00 Wednesday shows on Tuesday, in the calendar, the counts and the .ics export.

Do I need an account?

No. The pattern lives only in your browser (and in any share link you copy). There is no server and no sign-up.

Can this handle daylight saving time?

Yes. Dates are computed as calendar days, so the 23-hour and 25-hour DST days cannot skip or double a date. Shift times stay as wall-clock times.
