4 on 4 Off Calendar — Free Rotation Calendar for Shift Workers

The 4 on / 4 off is one of the most popular shift patterns in warehouses, factories, call centres and healthcare: work four shifts, rest four days, repeat every 8 days. Because the 8-day cycle is not a multiple of 7, your days off drift across the week — weekends off rotate automatically and fairly, without anyone having to ask.

How the 4 on / 4 off pattern works

  • Your repeating sequence is 8 days long: Day, Day, Day, Day, Off, Off, Off, Off.
  • Anchor the pattern to one date you are 100% sure about — for example “on 14 March I definitely worked a Day, day 1 of my pattern” — and every past and future date is fixed from that one fact.
  • Because 8 and 7 share no alignment, your 4 off-block lands on a different weekday each cycle, so every third or fourth cycle includes a full weekend off.

Frequently asked questions

How many days do I work per month on 4 on 4 off?

Roughly half of them. In a 30-day month you work 15 shifts; averaged over a year it is 182.5 shifts — 45.6 shifts per month on average.

Does 4 on 4 off give me weekends off?

Regularly, but not every week. Since the cycle is 8 days, the off-block slides forward one weekday each cycle, so a full weekend off comes round every few cycles.

Do I need to know a “start date”?

No — you need one anchor date you are 100% certain about, plus which day of the pattern that date was. From that single fact, every other date is computed.

Is this an employer scheduling tool?

No. ShiftCal is worker-side only: your pattern, your browser, no manager, no workplace data, no account. Always check against your official roster.

Can I put it in my phone calendar?

Yes — export a .ics file from the generator (one event per working shift, correct timezone handling across daylight saving) and import it into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.
